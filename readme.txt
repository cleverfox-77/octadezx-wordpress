24/7 Agentic AI Customer Support, 7-Step Multi-Step Order Lookup, Visual Photo Search & AI Phone Receptionist for WordPress & WooCommerce.

== Description ==

[OctaDezx](https://octadezx.com) is an enterprise-grade, multimodal AI customer support platform for WordPress and [WooCommerce stores](https://octadezx.com/woocommerce-chatbot).

* **7-Step Deterministic Multi-Step Inquiries:** Splits compound customer messages into verified steps ([see published safety benchmark](https://octadezx.com/benchmarks/multi-step-inquiries)).
* **Multimodal Vision & Voice:** Customers can upload product photos on web chat or WhatsApp ([Multimodal AI Agent](https://octadezx.com/multimodal-ai-agent)) or call a live interruptible phone line ([AI Receptionist](https://octadezx.com/ai-receptionist)).
* **Flat-Rate Pricing ($29/mo):** No $0.99 per-resolution fees ([OctaDezx Pricing](https://octadezx.com/pricing)).
